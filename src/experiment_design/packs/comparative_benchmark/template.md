# Comparative benchmark plan

## Hypotheses and decision rules
Map each supplied hypothesis to a claim, comparison and prespecified rule.

## Data and evaluation protocol
Specify dataset provenance, splits, contamination controls and immutable revisions.

## Experiment E<n>: <comparison>
Specify baselines, paired units, seed policy, equal compute, metrics and recording.

## Analysis and interpretation
Prespecify uncertainty, effect sizes, multiplicity and negative or inconclusive interpretations.

## Limitations and reproducibility
Disclose resource shortfalls, assumptions and the procedure needed to reproduce the comparison.
